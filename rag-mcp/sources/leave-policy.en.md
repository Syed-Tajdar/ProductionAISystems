# Working Time and Leave Policy

Palm Grove Realty, Human Resources. Policy HR-POL-04, version 3.0, effective 1 January 2026. Owner: HR Manager.

## 1. Purpose and scope

1.1 This policy sets out working hours, remote work and leave for all employees of Palm Grove Realty, including employees on probation.

1.2 Where the employment contract gives an employee more favourable terms, the contract applies.

## 2. Working hours

2.1 The standard working week is 40 hours, Monday to Friday, from 09:00 to 18:00 with a one hour lunch break.

2.2 Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00. Outside core hours employees may start between 07:30 and 10:00 and finish 9 hours later, if their manager agrees.

2.3 During Ramadan the working day is reduced by 2 hours for all employees.

2.4 Leasing and property staff who work on site follow a roster that includes Saturdays. They receive a weekday off for each Saturday worked.

## 3. Remote work

3.1 After passing probation, office-based employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week with their manager's approval.

3.2 Remote work is not available for reception, property officers or maintenance supervisors, because these roles need to be on site.

3.3 Employees working remotely must be reachable by phone and the team chat during core hours.

## 4. Leave entitlements

4.1 Employees are entitled to the following paid and unpaid leave:

| Leave type | Entitlement | Pay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Annual leave | 25 working days per year, accrued monthly | Full pay |
| Sick leave | First 15 days per year | Full pay |
| Sick leave | Next 30 days | Half pay |
| Maternity leave | 60 calendar days | 45 days full pay, 15 days half pay |
| Parental leave | 5 working days within 6 months of the birth | Full pay |
| Bereavement leave | 5 days for a spouse, 3 days for a parent, child or sibling | Full pay |
| Study leave | 10 working days per year for approved exams | Full pay |

4.2 Annual leave accrues from the first day of employment but can be taken only after probation, except in an emergency approved by HR.

4.3 Sick leave of more than one day needs a medical certificate, uploaded to the HR portal within 2 working days of returning.

## 5. Requesting leave

5.1 Request leave through the HR portal. Leave of 3 days or more must be requested at least 14 days in advance; shorter leave at least 2 working days in advance.

5.2 Managers answer leave requests within 3 working days. A request with no answer after 3 working days is escalated to HR automatically.

5.3 No more than two people from the same team may be on annual leave at the same time between 15 December and 10 January.

## 6. Carry-over and encashment

6.1 Up to 5 unused annual leave days can be carried over to the next year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March, after which they lapse.

6.2 Unused annual leave is paid out only when employment ends, at the basic daily salary.

## 7. Probation and notice

7.1 The probation period is 6 months. During probation, either side may end employment with 14 days notice.

7.2 After probation the notice period is 30 days, or as stated in the employment contract.

## 8. Public holidays

8.1 Employees receive the official public holidays announced for the private sector. Staff who work on a public holiday receive a replacement day off within 30 days.
