# Tenant Handbook

Palm Grove Realty, Palm Grove Residences, Dubai. Reference PGR-TH-2026, version 2.1, effective 1 March 2026.

## 1. About this handbook

1.1 This handbook explains how tenancies are managed at Palm Grove Residences. It applies to every residential unit leased through Palm Grove Realty.

1.2 If this handbook and your tenancy contract say different things, the tenancy contract applies.

## 2. Moving in

### 2.1 Handover inspection

2.1.1 On the handover day a property officer walks through the unit with you and records the condition of walls, floors, fittings and appliances in a handover report.

2.1.2 You have 7 days after handover to report any defect that is not in the report. Send it in writing to the maintenance team, with photos. Defects reported later are treated as damage during the tenancy.

### 2.2 Keys and access cards

2.2.1 Each unit receives two sets of keys, two building access cards and one parking tag.

2.2.2 A lost access card is replaced for AED 250. A lost parking tag is replaced for AED 150. If keys are lost, the lock cylinder is changed for AED 400.

### 2.3 Tenancy registration

2.3.1 Palm Grove Realty registers the tenancy contract with the government tenancy registration system within 5 working days of receiving the signed contract and a copy of your Emirates ID.

2.3.2 The registration fee of AED 220 is paid by the tenant and is added to the first invoice.

### 2.4 Utilities

2.4.1 You open the electricity and water account in your own name before handover. Bring the final bill and the clearance letter when you move out.

2.4.2 District cooling charges are included in the rent at Palm Grove Residences. Internet and TV are arranged by the tenant.

## 3. Rent and payments

### 3.1 Payment plans

3.1.1 Rent is paid in advance for the contract year. You can choose one of these plans:

| Plan | Payments per year | Price adjustment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Single payment | 1 cheque or transfer | 2% discount on annual rent |
| Two payments | 2 cheques | 1% discount |
| Standard | 4 cheques | No adjustment |
| Monthly | 12 cheques | 3% surcharge |

3.1.2 Post-dated cheques are collected at contract signing and deposited on their due date. Bank transfers must quote the unit number as the payment reference.

### 3.2 Security deposit

3.2.1 The security deposit is 5% of the annual rent for unfurnished units and 10% for furnished units. It is paid at signing and is not used for rent.

### 3.3 Late and returned payments

3.3.1 A returned cheque costs AED 500 and must be replaced within 7 days by a bank transfer or a certified cheque.

3.3.2 After two returned cheques in one contract year, Palm Grove Realty may ask for the remaining rent of that year in a single payment.

3.3.3 Rent paid by transfer more than 10 days late incurs a late payment fee of AED 300.

## 4. Maintenance

### 4.1 Reporting a problem

4.1.1 Report maintenance through the tenant app, by email to maintenance@palmgrove.example, or by calling the 24 hour hotline on 800 7256. Each request receives a reference number.

### 4.2 Response times

4.2.1 Requests are prioritised as follows:

| Priority | Examples | First response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Emergency | Water leak, power cut in the unit, lift entrapment, gas smell | Within 2 hours, 24/7 |
| Urgent | Air conditioning not cooling between May and October, no hot water | Within 24 hours |
| Routine | Dripping tap, door handle, minor electrical fault | Within 5 working days |

### 4.3 Tenant responsibilities

4.3.1 The tenant pays for minor repairs up to AED 300 per item, and for repairs caused by misuse.

4.3.2 Air conditioning filters must be cleaned every 3 months. Palm Grove Realty services the air conditioning units twice a year at no charge.

### 4.4 Access to the unit

4.4.1 Except in emergencies, the maintenance team gives at least 24 hours notice before entering the unit and visits between 08:00 and 18:00.

## 5. Building rules

### 5.1 Quiet hours

5.1.1 Quiet hours are from 22:00 to 07:00 every day. Loud music, drilling and moving furniture are not allowed during these hours.

### 5.2 Pets

5.2.1 Up to two pets are allowed per unit with written approval: cats, and dogs under 10 kg. Dogs must be on a lead in all shared areas. Pets are not allowed in the pool area or the gym.

### 5.3 Balconies

5.3.1 Laundry must not be visible from outside, and barbecues with an open flame are not allowed on balconies. Electric grills are allowed.

### 5.4 Parking

5.4.1 Each unit has one allocated parking bay. Visitors may use the visitor parking for up to 3 hours; longer stays need a visitor permit from reception.

### 5.5 Alterations

5.5.1 Painting, fixing shelves and any alteration need written approval. Approved works may only take place from Monday to Saturday, 09:00 to 18:00.

## 6. Renewal and moving out

### 6.1 Renewal

6.1.1 Palm Grove Realty sends renewal terms, including any change in rent, at least 90 days before the contract ends.

### 6.2 Notice to vacate

6.2.1 If you do not wish to renew, give written notice at least 60 days before the contract end date.

### 6.3 Early termination

6.3.1 Ending the contract early requires 60 days written notice and a penalty of two months rent. The penalty is waived if a new tenant approved by Palm Grove Realty takes over the unit within that period.

### 6.4 Move-out inspection and deposit refund

6.4.1 A move-out inspection compares the unit with the handover report. Normal wear and tear is not charged.

6.4.2 The deposit, minus any agreed deductions, is refunded within 30 days after the keys are returned and the final utility clearance letter is received.
