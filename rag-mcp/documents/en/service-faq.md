# Customer Service FAQ

Answers to the questions customers ask Palm Grove Realty most often. Updated 15 February 2026.

## 1. Opening hours and contact

### 1.1 When is the office open?

The sales and leasing office is open Monday to Friday from 09:00 to 18:00, and Saturday from 10:00 to 14:00. It is closed on Sundays and public holidays. The maintenance hotline, 800 7256, works 24 hours a day.

### 1.2 How can I reach you?

Call 04 555 0101, write to hello@palmgrove.example, or send a WhatsApp message to +971 50 000 0199. We reply to WhatsApp and email within one working day.

## 2. Renting a home

### 2.1 How do I book a viewing?

Book through the website, the app or WhatsApp. Viewings are available seven days a week from 09:00 to 20:00, and we confirm the time at least 24 hours before. Please bring your Emirates ID or passport to the viewing.

### 2.2 Which documents do I need to rent?

You need a copy of your passport, residence visa and Emirates ID. If the tenancy is in a company name, we also need the trade licence and the authorised signatory's Emirates ID.

### 2.3 What is the agency fee?

The agency fee for a new tenancy is 5% of the annual rent, with a minimum of AED 5,000, plus 5% VAT. There is no agency fee when you renew a tenancy with us.

### 2.4 How long does it take to sign a contract?

Once we have your documents, the cheques and the security deposit, the contract is usually signed within 2 working days. Tenancy registration follows within 5 working days.

### 2.5 Can I pay rent by credit card?

Yes. Card payments carry a 2.5% processing fee. Cheques and bank transfers have no fee.

## 3. Landlord services

### 3.1 Do you manage properties for owners?

Yes. We offer three management packages:

| Package | Fee | What is included |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lease Only | 50% of one month's rent, once | Marketing, viewings, tenant screening, contract and registration |
| Managed | 6% of annual rent | Lease Only, plus rent collection, maintenance coordination and renewals |
| Managed Plus | 8% of annual rent | Managed, plus quarterly inspections, utility handling and a yearly condition report |

### 3.2 How often do landlords receive their rent?

Rent collected under the Managed and Managed Plus packages is paid to the owner by the 10th of the month after the cheque clears, with a monthly statement.

## 4. Complaints and feedback

### 4.1 How do I make a complaint?

Write to complaints@palmgrove.example or use the feedback form in the app. We acknowledge every complaint within 1 working day and give a full answer within 5 working days.

### 4.2 What if I am not satisfied with the answer?

Ask for the complaint to be escalated. The Customer Experience Manager reviews it and replies within 10 working days.

## 5. Other questions

### 5.1 Are pets allowed?

It depends on the building. At Palm Grove Residences, cats and small dogs are allowed with written approval; see the Tenant Handbook for the rules.

### 5.2 Do you help with buying a property?

Yes. Our sales team handles off-plan and ready properties in Dubai and can introduce you to mortgage advisers. We do not charge buyers a fee for introductions to mortgage advisers.

### 5.3 Do you offer short-term rentals?

No. We only offer annual tenancies of 12 months or longer.
