# Residents' Amenities Guide

Shared facilities at Palm Grove Residences, Dubai, and how to book them. Available in English only. Updated 1 April 2026.

## 1. Swimming pool

1.1 The pool is open every day from 06:00 to 22:00. It is closed on Mondays from 06:00 to 10:00 for cleaning.

1.2 A lifeguard is on duty from 08:00 to 20:00. Outside these hours residents swim at their own risk.

1.3 Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult at all times. Glass, food and loud music are not allowed in the pool area.

## 2. Gym

2.1 The gym is open 24 hours a day and is entered with the building access card.

2.2 The minimum age is 16. Residents aged 14 and 15 may use the gym only with a parent present.

2.3 Personal trainers must be registered at reception before their first session. Each resident may bring one trainer at a time.

## 3. Barbecue area

3.1 The barbecue area on the podium is booked through the tenant app, up to 14 days ahead, in slots of 4 hours between 12:00 and 23:00.

3.2 A refundable deposit of AED 200 is charged for each booking and returned within 5 working days if the area is left clean.

3.3 Up to 15 guests are allowed per booking. Each household can book at most two slots a month.

## 4. Function room

4.1 The function room seats 40 people and costs AED 150 per hour, with a minimum booking of 2 hours.

4.2 Bookings are made at reception at least 3 working days ahead. Music must stop at 22:00.

## 5. Service lift for moving

5.1 Moving furniture in or out requires a service lift booking, made at reception at least 48 hours ahead.

5.2 Moves are allowed Monday to Saturday from 09:00 to 17:00. A refundable deposit of AED 500 covers damage to lifts and corridors.
