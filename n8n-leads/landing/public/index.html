Send a test enquiry

This is the contact form of Palm Grove Realty, a fictional Dubai property agency. Send it and see what the n8n workflow did with your enquiry: how urgent it is and why, whether it was a repeat, whether sales got an alert, and the reply email. The server sleeps when nobody uses it, so the first enquiry after a quiet spell takes 2 to 3 minutes.

What this demo shows

  1. An AI model reads each enquiry, in English or Arabic, and rates it hot, warm or cold with a one-line reason.
  2. A repeat enquiry is matched by phone or email and updates the saved lead instead of adding a second one. Hot leads alert sales on Telegram.
  3. The reply email is written for real and shown on this page instead of sent, so this public form can't be used to send messages to someone else's address. In a real setup the same step sends it.
